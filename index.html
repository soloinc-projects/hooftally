Hooftally

The livestock record book where the sale posts to your books.

A records book for 5 to 50 cattle, sheep, goats or pigs kept by one person on one phone with no signal needed — not for dairies, pedigree herds or 500-head ranches.

Your records stay on your phone: no account, nothing uploaded.

Three things you can check yourself

  • Mark it sold, and the books change too. Sell tag 42 for $1,250 with a $60 sale fee: the month shows +$1,250.00 in livestock sales and −$60.00 in sale fees on the same screen, and the herd is one head smaller. Undo adds reversing lines and keeps the sale's own, so your books show both.
  • Works with the phone in Airplane Mode. No sign-in, no account, no cloud. Make a backup file whenever you like and keep it in your own storage.
  • $24.99 a year with the first 14 days free, or $69.99 once. If a yearly plan ends, everything stays readable and CSV, PDF and backup export keep working.

What else is in it

  • One search field for a tag, name, species, buyer or note.
  • Farm expenses with a date, a note and, if you like, the animal.
  • Treatments recorded as the label reads, with the “do not sell before” date you type. Hooftally never works that date out; the label or your vet decides.
  • Import animals from a spreadsheet (CSV); export animals and the ledger as CSV.
  • Dark mode, the largest text sizes, VoiceOver and TalkBack.
