Privacy policy

Hooftally, by 8086 LLC. Effective October 25, 2026.

Hooftally collects no data. We, the developer, never receive your records, your name or anything about how you use the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Your animals, sales, treatments, expenses and the ledger, kept in the app's own storage on your phone.
  • Any buyer's name or note you type into a sale. It is never sent anywhere.
  • A short list of what the app did on this phone (its last 500 events, for example “backup made” or “sale undone”), which you can read and clear under About → Diagnostics. It holds no names, notes, tags, prices or searches, and it is never sent to us; it leaves the phone only if you share it yourself.
  • The age range your phone's app store reports at first launch (Apple's Declared Age Range, Google Play's Age Signals), used on the phone only. Hooftally has nothing that is unsuitable for any age, so it shows the same app to everyone.

What leaves your phone, and only when you do it

  • A backup file, CSV or PDF you choose to make and share. It goes where you send it (Files, email, a drive) and nowhere else.
  • Buying or restoring a plan, which goes through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies; we receive no payment details.
  • If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is turned on, it may include the app's data, under your Apple or Google account. We never see it.

What we never do

  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, camera, photos, contacts or microphone.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete and nothing held by us. To delete everything, delete the app from your phone; that removes all its records. Backup files, CSVs or PDFs you made are yours, wherever you saved them; delete them there.

Children

Hooftally is a farm record book for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with its date. Questions: support@8086.llc.
