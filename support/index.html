Common questions

Do I need an account or a signal?

No. There is no account, and the app works in Airplane Mode. Buying or restoring a plan needs a connection to the App Store or Google Play.

What happens when I mark an animal sold?

Before you post, the screen shows the lines it will add. When you post, the animal leaves the herd and the price and the sale fee are added to that month's books as separate lines.

I got a sale wrong. How do I fix it?

Open the sold animal and tap Undo this sale. Hooftally adds reversing lines, keeps the sale's own lines and the reason you give, and returns the animal to the herd. You can then sell it again at the right price.

How do I move to a new phone, or keep a copy?

Settings → Back up now makes one backup file. Save it in Files, a drive or email. On the new phone, install Hooftally and choose Settings → Restore from a backup.

Can I bring my spreadsheet?

Yes. Settings → Import animals (CSV). Lines that cannot be read are listed with the reason, and nothing in your books is changed by an import.

How do I give my accountant the month?

Books → Month-end sheet, pick the dates, then share it as a PDF (one page per species plus a summary) or a CSV.

Does Hooftally work out withdrawal dates?

No, never. You type the label's wording and the “do not sell before” date yourself, and the app shows them as typed. The label or your vet decides; this is not veterinary advice.

What do the plans cost, and what if I stop paying?

$24.99 a year with the first 14 days free, or $69.99 once (U.S. prices). If a yearly plan ends, your records stay readable and CSV, PDF and backup export keep working; adding new records needs a plan.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

Restore purchases is on the plan screen. To cancel a yearly plan: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple's refund request page, or Google Play's refund page.
